### ChronoSquares Platform (SaaS)

#### Product purpose

ChronoSquares is a shared client-delivery platform for managing the complete relationship between a service provider, the client, and any other parties involved.

- Give every party one reliable source of truth
- Make responsibilities, progress, costs, and decisions visible
- Automate repetitive handovers and administration
- Provide clients with a professional and reassuring experience
- Let providers manage the detailed operation without overwhelming the client

#### Initial niche

- Service-based agencies
- Approximately 5 to 50 active clients
- Retainer-based or project-based work
- Manual or fragmented onboarding and delivery processes
- Teams currently relying on email, spreadsheets, forms, documents, and disconnected project tools

#### Parties and access

Each person receives a role-based view containing only the information and actions relevant to them.

- **Service provider:** oversees clients, projects, finances, risks, tasks, and internal operations
- **Client:** reviews progress, completes requests, approves work, accesses files, and sees agreed costs
- **Contractor or partner:** receives assigned work, deadlines, files, and relevant communication
- **Other stakeholders:** can be invited for specific approvals, deliverables, or stages

#### The 7 Squares

The seven core Squares represent the full service lifecycle.

1. **Lead Square** — enquiries, qualification, discovery, proposals, and quotations
2. **Onboarding Square** — intake forms, contracts, initial payments, document collection, and kickoff
3. **Project Square** — milestones, tasks, responsibilities, dependencies, and progress
4. **Communication Square** — updates, questions, decisions, approvals, and meeting records
5. **Finance Square** — costs, budgets, invoices, payments, and financial visibility
6. **Files Square** — contracts, assets, working documents, versions, and final deliverables
7. **Care Square** — support, feedback, issue resolution, renewals, and ongoing client success

#### Client lifecycle

1. A lead submits an enquiry or is added by the provider.
2. The provider qualifies the lead and sends a proposal or quotation.
3. The client accepts, signs the contract, pays, and completes onboarding requirements.
4. ChronoSquares creates the shared workspace, project structure, tasks, milestones, and requests.
5. All parties track delivery, communicate, approve work, and exchange files in one place.
6. The project closes with final delivery, payment, feedback, and an organised record.
7. The relationship moves into support, renewal, or another project without restarting the process.

#### Shared workspace model

- One provider can manage multiple organisations, clients, and projects
- One client can access multiple active and completed services
- A project can involve internal team members, clients, contractors, and external partners
- Permissions separate private provider operations from shared client information
- Every action contributes to a clear history of what happened and when

#### Workflow Layer

The Workflow Layer connects the Squares and moves information through the service lifecycle.

- Trigger contracts, payment requests, forms, tasks, reminders, and status changes
- Create the correct workspace when a proposal is accepted
- Notify the right party when an action or approval is required
- Prevent stages from progressing until required conditions are complete
- Connect external tools where businesses still need them

#### AI Architect

The AI Architect helps providers configure and operate their service systems.

- Build workflows from a plain-language description of a service
- Generate onboarding checklists, forms, milestones, and client updates
- Summarise progress, risks, overdue actions, and recent decisions
- Help users locate contracts, costs, files, and previous communication
- Recommend improvements based on repeated bottlenecks

#### Business model

- Subscription based primarily on provider organisation size, active clients, or both
- Clients and invited external parties access shared workspaces without purchasing a full provider account
- Higher plans unlock advanced workflows, permissions, reporting, integrations, and AI usage
- Optional implementation, migration, and custom automation delivered through FlowGrid

#### Initial MVP

Focus on one complete workflow rather than building every Square at full depth.

- Provider dashboard
- Client portal
- Intake form and document collection
- Proposal, contract, and initial payment status
- Project milestones and shared progress
- Tasks, approvals, files, and updates
- Basic cost and invoice tracking
- Automated reminders and handovers

### FlowGrid

FlowGrid is the service arm that designs and implements client-delivery systems for businesses while ChronoSquares develops into the scalable software product.

### Brand role

- **ChronoSquares:** the platform clients and service providers use

- FlowGrid generates early revenue, validates demand, and reveals which workflows ChronoSquares should standardise
- Suitable FlowGrid clients can later migrate from bespoke systems into ChronoSquares

### Niche
